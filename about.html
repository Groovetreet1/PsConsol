Our story

Built for the love of play.

PS5Consol is a fan-made concept born from late nights, big screens and the feeling that games can take us somewhere else entirely.

How it started

From a sketch to a universe.

It began with a single sketch: a console that looked like it belonged in a sci-fi film, paired with a controller that could make you feel the rain.

Today PS5Consol is a small design studio of players, illustrators and engineers. We build the feeling of pressing start for the first time.
